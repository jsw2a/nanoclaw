Our Story

From the workshops of Puebla

Talavera has been made the same way for more than four centuries — clay thrown, dried, glazed, and painted entirely by hand before a second firing fixes the color forever. We work directly with a handful of family workshops, buying small batches and paying fairly, so the craft and the people who keep it alive can thrive.

Because each piece is painted freehand, yours will carry the small, lovely irregularities of a human hand. That's not a flaw — it's the signature.

Living With It

How to care for your pottery

Wash gently

Hand-wash with mild soap to keep the glaze and colors vivid for generations.

Use it daily

Our glazes are lead-free and food-safe — these pieces are made for the table, not the shelf.

Mind the heat

Oven-safe to 350°F. Avoid sudden temperature changes, as with any fine ceramic.
