From the workshops of Puebla

Talavera has been made the same way for four centuries — clay thrown, dried, glazed, and painted entirely by hand before a second firing fixes the color forever. We work directly with family workshops, in small batches, paid fairly.

Because each piece is painted freehand, yours carries the small, lovely irregularities of a human hand. That's not a flaw — it's the signature.
